# Writing for SEO (and AI answers)

Pairs with the **`seo` pack** (technical SEO). Here: how the *words* rank without
sounding like they were written for a crawler. Clarity first; SEO is how the right
humans find clear copy.

## Start from intent
- Identify the **search intent** behind the primary keyword (informational,
  commercial, transactional, navigational) and write to **that** job. Match the
  format searchers expect (guide, comparison, how-to, product page).

## Place terms naturally
- Primary term in the **title**, the **H1**, and the **first paragraph** — worded
  the way a human would say it. Use natural **variations/synonyms** and related
  entities; don't repeat the exact phrase mechanically. **Never keyword-stuff.**

## Structure for snippets and AI
- **Answer-first**: give the direct answer in the opening, then expand. This wins
  featured snippets **and** gets cited by AI Overviews / ChatGPT / Perplexity.
- Descriptive **H2/H3 that mirror real questions**; use **lists, tables, and FAQ**
  where they genuinely help (they extract cleanly for AI).
- Keep facts self-contained and specific (units, dates, definitions).

## Metadata (always supply)
- **Title** (~50–60 chars): primary term + brand, distinctive term first.
- **Meta description** (~150–160): a compelling, accurate summary with the term —
  written to earn the click, not to stuff keywords.

## Links & depth
- Add **intentional internal links** with descriptive anchors to related pages.
- Cover the topic with enough depth to be the best answer — but no padding;
  length follows the intent, not a word-count target.

## E-E-A-T (credibility)
- Show experience/expertise: named author, real examples, original data, sources,
  and visible dates. Consistent entity naming helps engines (and AI) trust you.

## The rule
If SEO guidance and human clarity ever conflict, **clarity wins** — a page that
reads well and answers the question is what both Google and AI reward now.
