# Copywriter — Tech & Innovation — Engineering Pack

The knowledge standard for **professional, human-sounding copy** aimed at
technology & innovation audiences — that also **ranks**. It feeds the
**`copywriter` agent** (and docs/frontend) via the Engineering Contract, and
pairs with the **`seo` pack**: the agent executes, these packs hold the standard.

## What it covers
- **[policies.md](policies.md)** — non-negotiable rules (truth, human voice, SEO,
  one CTA).
- **[voice-and-tone.md](voice-and-tone.md)** — how to sound like a real person.
- **[anti-patterns.md](anti-patterns.md)** — the **AI tells & clichés** to never write.
- **[frameworks.md](frameworks.md)** — persuasion structures (AIDA, PAS, headlines, CTAs).
- **[tech-domain.md](tech-domain.md)** — fluency in the tech universe (AI, cloud,
  SaaS, devtools, startups, security).
- **[seo-writing.md](seo-writing.md)** — writing for search **and** AI answers
  (pairs with the `seo` pack).
- **[patterns.md](patterns.md)** — repeatable moves that work.
- **[architecture.md](architecture.md)** — content strategy, briefs, editing loop.
- **[context.md](context.md)** — the compact brief injected into the agent.

## The bar
Copy is done when **a knowledgeable human would believe a knowledgeable human
wrote it**, the tech is **accurate**, it moves the reader toward **one action**,
and it's built to **rank and be cited**.

Packs are **knowledge only** — no code, no execution.
