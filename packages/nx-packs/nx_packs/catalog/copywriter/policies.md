# Copywriting policies (non-negotiable)

## Truth first
- **Never fabricate** specs, metrics, benchmarks, quotes, case studies, or
  capabilities. If it isn't verified, don't claim it — soften or cut.
- No hype that the product can't back up. Credibility compounds; overclaiming
  burns it once.

## Sound human
- Write in an active voice with **varied sentence rhythm** (mix short and long).
- Use **concrete specifics** (names, numbers you can verify, real examples) over
  abstractions. Take a clear point of view.
- Contractions and plain words where natural. Read it aloud — if it sounds like a
  press release or a bot, rewrite it.
- **Never** use the AI tells / clichés listed in `anti-patterns.md`.

## Know the domain
- Use precise, current tech terminology; match the audience's level. Explain a
  concept the first time only if the reader needs it — don't over-explain to
  experts or under-explain to newcomers.
- No buzzword salad ("revolutionary AI-powered synergy"). One real idea beats ten
  empty adjectives.

## Persuade with structure
- One **hook** that earns the next sentence. One **idea per section**. Scannable
  subheads. Exactly **one primary CTA** per piece (repeat it, don't multiply it).
- Lead with the reader's problem/outcome, not the company.

## Optimize for SEO (pairs with the `seo` pack)
- Match **search intent**; put the primary term naturally in the **title, H1, and
  first paragraph**; use descriptive **H2/H3** that mirror real queries.
- **Answer-first** so snippets and AI answers can quote you; add intentional
  **internal links**; always supply a **title (~50–60)** and **meta description
  (~150–160)**.
- Never keyword-stuff or write for robots at the expense of the human. If SEO and
  clarity conflict, clarity wins.

## Brand & language
- Respect the brand voice and the audience's language (PT-BR / EN as specified).
  Consistency across pieces.

## Governance
- Nothing ships that violates this file or `anti-patterns.md`. Technical claims are
  **verified**, not assumed (see `checklists.md`).
